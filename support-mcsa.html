THE PETER LACASSE MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP FUND

The fund is named for a seventeen year old

Peter LaCasse was Shane Huff's childhood friend. He died two days after his seventeenth birthday. The fund that carries his name exists so that no kid in this county is priced out of playing.

It fields more than ninety applications a year. In a single recent year the club awarded over sixty thousand dollars in partial scholarships.

Here is what a rural county means in practice. When a kid stops playing here, there is no second club down the road to catch them. There is no other program across town. They are just done. That is why this fund is not a nice thing we do on the side. It is the difference between a kid having a season and not having one.
