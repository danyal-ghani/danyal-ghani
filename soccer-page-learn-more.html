Indoor, small-sided, and fast. Futsal runs in Ukiah, Willits, and on the Coast, and it does more for a player's close control than almost anything else we run.

CAMPS

Seasonal camps for players who want extra touches outside their regular season. Open to everyone, not just select players.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

NOBODY GETS LOCKED IN.

Most clubs sort kids into teams at age eleven and leave them there. We train in pods, not locked teams, so a player can earn their way into a different group week to week. Select team practice players play their league games in DSL. A player can train with select staff and play their games locally.

Development is not linear and we do not pretend it is. Our job is to grow confident, adaptable players, not to chase trophies at U11.

ACCESS

COST SHOULD NOT BE THE REASON.

The Peter LaCasse Memorial Scholarship Fund fields more than ninety applications a year. Scholarships are partial and come with a commitment to finish the season. If cost is a barrier for your family, ask.
